# GPD AI Bingo: presenter script and launch post

## Presenter script (about 40 seconds)

> One thing that stood out at Feedforward was that AI leaders are experimenting themselves. They're trying new things, discovering what works, and developing fluency through experience.
>
> We thought it would be fun to bring some of that spirit to GPD. So here's GPD AI Bingo. *(show the card)*
>
> Every square is something you can try with AI. Click one and you get a short explanation, a prompt you can copy straight into your approved AI tool, and what good looks like.
>
> Some squares take five minutes, like turning messy notes into three clear actions. Others are a real stretch, like turning your best prompt into a reusable AI assistant, or redesigning a workflow with AI on the team.
>
> Whether you're new to AI or experimenting with agents, there's something here for you. Pick one square you've never tried and give it a go. Five in a row is Bingo, but one square is a win.

**On-screen tips**

- Open the site on the main card. The headline and sticky note carry the message.
- If you have 10 extra seconds, click **Surprise me** live. It's a nice moment.
- Keep the room's attention on the card, not on the activity pages.

## Teams / email post

**Subject / title:** What's something you haven't tried with AI yet?

> At Feedforward, one theme kept coming up: the AI leaders getting the most value are the ones experimenting hands-on.
>
> So we made **GPD AI Bingo**: 24 small AI experiments on one card, from 5-minute quick wins to ambitious builds.
>
> **How it works**
> 1. Open the card: **[paste the hosted link here]**
> 2. Click any square that catches your eye. Not sure where to start? Hit **Surprise me**.
> 3. Copy the prompt into your company-approved AI tool and try it.
> 4. Click **"I tried it — mark complete."** Five in a row is Bingo!
>
> Squares are marked **Explore** (quick wins), **Level Up** (AI as a thinking partner) or **Go Further** (agents, building, workflow redesign). The middle row is all quick wins, so anyone can get Bingo.
>
> There are no accounts, no tracking and no leaderboard. Progress is saved only in your own browser. Prefer paper? There's a **Print / Save PDF** button.
>
> A few ground rules: use approved AI tools, keep confidential information out unless your tool is approved for it, and review what AI gives you before you use it.
>
> Start anywhere. One square is a win. Tell us what you tried!
